Summary Format

Same content — three different ways to present it. Which is easiest to scan?

A

TL;DR + Expandable Full Summary

2-line plain-English headline up front. Full detail hidden behind "Read more" — there when you need it, out of the way when you don't.

Summary
TL;DR
RBI requires all NBFCs to remove 7 delisted entries from their sanctions screening databases immediately. This is an update to an existing KYC obligation — no new policy changes needed.
Full analysis (8 sentences) ▼ Read more
The Reserve Bank of India has issued an update under the Master Direction on Know Your Customer (KYC), 2016, specifically addressing amendments to the UNSC 1267/1989 Consolidated Sanctions List...

Pros

  • Fastest to scan
  • Full depth still accessible
  • Works for quick daily review

Cons

  • Requires AI to generate two outputs (TL;DR + full)
  • Users may skip the full summary
B

Structured Bullets + Brief Intro ★ Recommended

1–2 sentence context-setter, then 4–5 scannable bullets covering What, Who, Why, Deadline, Risk. Full depth without the wall of text.

Summary

RBI has amended the Master Direction on KYC to reflect the latest UNSC sanctions list update, requiring regulated entities to immediately remove 7 delisted entries from their screening databases.

WHAT Removal of 7 UNSC-delisted individuals from the ISIL / Al-Qaida consolidated sanctions list, effective immediately.
WHO All NBFCs, commercial banks, UCBs, RRBs, and other RBI-regulated entities with KYC / sanctions screening obligations.
WHY Under Section 51A of UAPA 1967, entities must mirror UNSC list changes in real time. Continued blocking of delisted persons is itself a compliance failure.
DEADLINE Immediate — no grace period specified. Database update and compliance log entry required within days of circular date.
IMPACT Operational only — update your screening vendor or internal DB. No new policy or product changes required.

Pros

  • Eyes go straight to what matters
  • No information lost
  • AI can structure output as JSON labels
  • Scales well — longer items just add bullets

Cons

  • Slightly more AI prompt complexity
C

Short Paragraphs with Micro-Labels

Full prose but broken into 3 short labelled paragraphs: Context · Obligation · Impact. Feels more like a legal brief, easier to skim than one block.

Summary
CONTEXT

RBI has updated the Master Direction on KYC 2016 to reflect the latest UNSC sanctions list changes. Seven individuals previously listed under the ISIL / Al-Qaida sanctions regime have been officially delisted by the UN Security Council, and the RBI is requiring all regulated entities to mirror this update immediately.

OBLIGATION

Under Section 51A of the UAPA 1967, NBFCs and all RBI-regulated entities are mandated to maintain real-time alignment with UNSC sanctions lists. Failure to remove delisted entries is itself a compliance violation — potentially causing wrongful blocking of legitimate customers. No new policies are introduced; this is an operational update to an existing control.

IMPACT

The action required is limited to updating your sanctions screening database and evidencing the change in your compliance register. No product, process, or policy changes are needed. Priority is immediate — there is no stated grace period in the circular.

Pros

  • Familiar to legal/compliance readers
  • Full prose — no information compressed
  • Easy to scan with labels

Cons

  • Still text-heavy vs. B
  • Less structured for quick daily triage
